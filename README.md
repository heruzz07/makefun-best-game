EMBERDRIFT — pilot a salvage skiff through a debris field under a ringed gas giant. Collect glowing embers to charge your core: core is your score multiplier, your nova fuel, and your overdrive fuel, so spending it is always a choice. Spend 30 core on a nova blast to clear the rocks, or hold SHIFT to overdrive — burn core for speed and a bigger combo step. Golden embers are worth 5; shield cells block one hit. Every 100 embers forges a star: pick 1 of 3 upgrades (hull, nova, magnet, thrusters, combo, core). Forge 5 stars to light the Beacon and win. Chasers hunt you, weavers block the lanes, splitters break apart, mines arm and detonate, sweepers cross the field. An original game, built from scratch in vanilla JavaScript (canvas 2D, zero libraries, zero network) by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best game" bounty.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Mouse or WASD/arrows to fly, Space or click for nova, hold SHIFT for overdrive, 1/2/3 (or click) to pick an upgrade, P to pause, M to mute.
