EMBERDRIFT — pilot a salvage skiff through a debris field. Collect glowing embers to charge your core: core is both your score-multiplier fuel and your nova fuel. Spend 30 core on a nova blast to clear the rocks, or hoard it and let your combo pay. Every 100 embers forges a star: a new wave, +1 max hull, +1 hull. An original game, built from scratch in vanilla JavaScript (canvas 2D, zero libraries, zero network) by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best game" bounty.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Mouse or WASD/arrows to fly, Space or click for nova, P to pause, M to mute.
